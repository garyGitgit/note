- This has been called 'Civil War' because this debate includes miners, full noder and even ordinal stakeholders.
- The topic for the debate comes from so-called BRC-20 token and Ordinals.
- The total market capitalization of BRC-20 tokens surpassed $1 billion.
- Some people argue that ordinals can increase the demands for block spaces, and finally it will lead to benefits for miners.
- Result 1. High demands for blockspaces results in mempool congestion - pending transactions - in full nodes.
- Result 2. High demands for blockspaces results in high transaction fee: even the fee exceeds the reward from mining
