\subsubsection{Ground Truth Construction}For each query episode $q$, prior episodes $p$ (created before $q$) are labeled relevant under hierarchical criteria: (1) \textbf{explicit reference}: $q$'s JIRA thread cites $p$ as related prior art (strongest); (2) \textbf{structural recurrence}: $q$ and $p$ share the same design block and the same resolved root-cause category, with matching regex error type as a further strengthening signal. Of the 907 ground-truth query sets, 12 derive solely from explicit references, 850 solely from block+root-cause agreement, and 45 from both.
